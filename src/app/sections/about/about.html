Our Story

Founded in 2012, Solv started as a small team of three developers with a big dream: to make enterprise-grade software accessible to businesses of all sizes. Over the past decade, we've grown into a 35+ person team delivering innovative solutions across healthcare, fintech, e-commerce, and education.

Our philosophy is simple — we don't just write code. We build long-term partnerships, understand your business deeply, and craft solutions that scale with your ambitions.
